Transparency First

How Decanting
Works

Every decant carries a complete chain of custody from the retailer's sealed box to your hands. No middlemen, no mystery — just verifiable provenance at every step.

01

Authentic Sourcing

We purchase sealed bottles exclusively from authorized brand retailers. Each is photographed, batch-coded, and entered into our traceability system before any seal is broken.

02

Precision Decanting

Trained specialists fill medical-grade glass atomizers to exact weight in a clean-room environment. Each decant is labeled with its source bottle's batch number immediately after filling.

03

Sealed & Traced

Your order arrives with a traceability card showing the original bottle, pour date, and your decant's sequence number — scan it any time to verify what you received.
