What is a Decant?

A decant is a portion of an authentic, sealed fragrance bottle divided into smaller samples. We source every drop from verified retailers, pour in medical-grade atomizers, and trace each decant back to its original bottle. This means you get the exact fragrance at a fraction of the cost with complete provenance documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your decants authentic?
+
100% yes. Every decant is sourced from sealed bottles purchased from authorized retailers. Each decant is traced back to the original bottle with a traceability number, batch code, and pour date. We guarantee authenticity or your money back.
How long do decants last?
+
Decants last as long as full bottles — 3 to 5 years or more if stored properly (cool, dark place). Since they're in sealed atomizers, they maintain fragrance integrity until the spray valve is broken.
Can I return or exchange a decant?
+
Yes! If a decant arrives damaged or if the fragrance doesn't match the description, we offer a full refund or exchange within 30 days of purchase. For opened decants, we accept returns based on our return policy.
How do you source fragrances?
+
We partner directly with authorized distributors and retailers of major brands. Every bottle is purchased new, sealed, and verified before any portion is decanted. We never purchase from grey markets or counterfeit sources.
What if I want a fragrance you don't have?
+
We're constantly adding new fragrances based on customer demand. You can request a specific fragrance, and we'll do our best to source it. Submit requests through our brand request form.
